演示要点 / Key points
T
温度决定分子速度
Higher T = faster molecules = more collisions
V
体积改变活塞位置
Larger V = piston rises = lower pressure
P
压强来自分子碰撞
P = nRT/V — pressure from wall collisions
n
物质的量决定分子数
More moles = more particles = higher pressure
