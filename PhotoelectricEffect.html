演示要点 / Key points
hf
光子能量取决于频率
Photon energy depends on frequency, not intensity.
φ
存在截止频率（红限）
Threshold frequency exists. Below it, no electrons are emitted.
K
光强影响电子数量非动能
Intensity affects number of electrons, not their kinetic energy.
V₀
遏止电压测最大动能
Stopping voltage measures max kinetic energy: K = eV₀.
